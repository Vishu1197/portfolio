About the Researcher

Vishal Chanda is a PhD Researcher specializing in Bioinformatics, Nanotechnology, and Virology.

His work integrates RNA-seq analytics, molecular simulations, and experimental virology to understand dengue virus pathogenesis and thrombocytopenia. He designs lipid-based nanocarriers, performs computational drug discovery, and validates results using in-vitro virology techniques — bridging AI-driven modeling with experimental biology to build next-generation antiviral therapeutics.
